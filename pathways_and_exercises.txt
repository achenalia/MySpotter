Pathways and Tracks for Senior People
1. Balance Exercises
Pathway name: Balance and Stability
Pathway length: 4 weeks
Frequency: 2–3 sessions per week
Track duration: Approximately 6–10 minutes
Goal: Improve balance, stability, and confidence while standing and walking.
Pathway Progression
Week 1: Learn the exercises with appropriate support.
Week 2: Practice controlled movements and improve consistency.
Week 3: Gradually increase practice time if comfortable.
Week 4: Combine both exercises into a regular balance routine.
Track A: Balance Basics
Duration: 6–8 minutes
Difficulty: Beginner
Description: Introduces two basic balance exercises to help users practice stability and controlled movement.
Heel-to-toe walk: 2 minutes, including rest.
Stand on One Foot: 2 minutes total, alternating sides.
Rest and transitions: 2–4 minutes.
Exercise videos:
Heel-to-toe walk: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_GKdFf3qv4 (0:30–0:40)
Stand on One Foot: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QT5d4tTXW6U
Back Injury Alternatives:
N/A for this track
Track B: Steady Steps
Duration: 6–8 minutes
Difficulty: Beginner
Description: Repeats the balance movements with an emphasis on controlled steps and steady positioning.
Gentle practice of the heel-to-toe walk: 2 minutes.
Stand on One Foot: 2 minutes total, alternating sides.
Rest and transitions: 2–4 minutes.
Track C: Balance Practice
Duration: 8–10 minutes
Difficulty: Beginner
Description: Combines both exercises into a slightly longer session to encourage consistency and confidence.
Heel-to-toe walk: 2–3 minutes, including rest.
Stand on One Foot: 2–3 minutes total, alternating sides.
Repeat the movements at a comfortable pace: 2 minutes.
Rest and transitions: 2 minutes.
2. Strength Training
Pathway name: Upper-Body Strength
Pathway length: 6 weeks
Frequency: 2 sessions per week initially, with recovery between sessions
Track duration: Approximately 10–15 minutes
Goal: Maintain upper-body strength for everyday activities such as reaching, pushing, and supporting body movement.
Pathway Progression
Weeks 1–2: Learn each movement and practice with an appropriate level of effort.
Weeks 3–4: Build consistency and control while allowing enough recovery.
Weeks 5–6: Gradually increase repetitions or practice time when comfortable and appropriate.
Track A: Strength Foundations
Duration: 10–12 minutes
Description: Introduces the three selected strength exercises at a manageable pace.
Wall push-ups: 2 minutes, including rest.
Overhead Arm Raise: 2 minutes, including rest.
Chair Dip Strength Exercise: 2 minutes, including rest.
Rest and transitions: 4–6 minutes.

Overhead Arm Raise: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VPiawRHRpmY
Chair Dip Strength Exercise: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVRcXDEdC5U
Back Injury Alternatives
Pendulum Swings: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zY5nq68IxwA 
Supported Shoulder Range of Motion: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jt4CB1xk3YU
Track B: Controlled Strength
Duration: 10–12 minutes
Description: Focuses on controlled movement and appropriate rest between exercises.
Overhead Arm Raise: 2 minutes, including rest.
Wall push-ups: 2 minutes, including rest.
Chair Dip Strength Exercise: 2 minutes, including rest.
Rest and transitions: 4–6 minutes.
Track C: Full Strength Routine
Duration: 12–15 minutes
Description: Combines all three movements into one session, with enough time to practice carefully and recover between exercises.
Wall push-ups: 2 minutes.
Overhead Arm Raise: 2 minutes.
Chair Dip Strength Exercise: 2 minutes.
Repeat selected movements at a comfortable level: 2–3 minutes.
Rest and transitions: 4–6 minutes.
3. Flexibility and Stretching
Pathway name: Flexibility and Comfortable Movement
Pathway length: 4 weeks
Frequency: 3–5 sessions per week, as comfortable
Track duration: Approximately 8–12 minutes
Goal: Maintain comfortable movement in the ankles, back, and backs of the legs.
Pathway Progression
Week 1: Learn each stretch and find a comfortable range of motion.
Week 2: Practice holding stretches gently without bouncing.
Week 3: Combine the three stretches into a routine.
Week 4: Establish a consistent stretching habit.
Track A: Gentle Stretching
Duration: 8–10 minutes
Description: Introduces the three stretches to help users practice comfortable movement.
Ankle Stretch: 2 minutes, including both sides if applicable.
Back Stretch: 2 minutes.
Back Of Leg Stretch: 2 minutes, including both sides if applicable.
Rest and transitions: 2–4 minutes.
Exercise videos:
Ankle Stretch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=87BeiyTFZyU
Back Stretch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FpIfMyfpCk0
Back Of Leg Stretch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=guOj99WPZnw
Track B: Lower-Body Flexibility
Duration: 8–10 minutes
Description: Emphasizes the ankle and back-of-leg stretches while keeping movements gentle and controlled.
Ankle Stretch: 2 minutes.
Back Of Leg Stretch: 3 minutes, including both sides if applicable.
Back Stretch: 2 minutes.
Rest and transitions: 1–3 minutes.
Track C: Complete Stretching Routine
Duration: 10–12 minutes
Description: Combines all three stretches into a longer session that users can follow as part of their regular routine.
Ankle Stretch: 2 minutes.
Back Stretch: 2–3 minutes.
Back Of Leg Stretch: 3 minutes.
Repeat any comfortable stretch if desired: 1 minute.
Rest and transitions: 2–3 minutes.

Pathway name: Everyday Movement and Injury Prevention
Pathway length: 4 weeks
Frequency: 3 sessions per week; choose a different track each session
Track duration: Approximately 8–12 minutes
Goal: Practice everyday habits that may help reduce the chance of minor sprains, muscle strains, and repetitive-use irritation. This pathway is for general wellness, not treatment for an existing injury.
Pathway Progression Injuries for Typical People
Week 1: Learn the movements and find a comfortable pace.
Week 2: Repeat the tracks with steady, controlled movements.
Week 3: Practice the habits during everyday tasks, such as carrying groceries or taking work breaks.
Week 4: Choose the habits you want to keep using each week.
Track A: Ankle Balance Basics
Duration: 8–10 minutes
Description: Practices gentle ankle movement and supported balance.
Easy walk or march: 2 minutes.
Ankle circles while seated: 1 minute per side.
Supported calf raises: 2 sets of 10.
Supported single-leg balance: up to 30 seconds per side, repeat as comfortable.
Rest and transitions: 1–2 minutes.
Exercise guide: AAOS Foot and Ankle Conditioning Program includes calf raises and supported single-leg balance.
Track B: Back-Friendly Everyday Lifting
Duration: 8–12 minutes
Description: Practices setting up for a light lift and moving without rushing.
Easy walk or march: 2 minutes.
Practice bending at the hips and knees without lifting anything: 2 minutes.
Practice picking up and setting down a light object: 3–4 minutes.
Practice keeping the object close and turning by moving your feet: 2 minutes.
Rest and transitions: 1–2 minutes.
Movement guide: MedlinePlus lifting tips covers staying close to the object, lifting slowly, and avoiding twisting while carrying.
Track C: Repetitive-Movement Reset
Duration: 8–10 minutes
Description: Helps users notice repetitive tasks and build in brief movement breaks.
Check posture and adjust the work area: 2 minutes.
Gentle shoulder rolls: 1 minute.
Gentle wrist and finger movements: 2 minutes.
Practice a short break, then plan when to vary tasks or take another break: 2–3 minutes.
Rest and transitions: 1–2 minutes.
